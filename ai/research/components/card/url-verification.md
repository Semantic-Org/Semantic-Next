## Component Definition

**Card**: A container for grouping related content and actions about a single subject, often with a header, body, and footer.

**Key Distinction**:
- Segment = Basic container primitive (Paper/Box/Panel)  ← Component #5
- **Card = Structured component with header/media/actions** ← Component #6 (this research)
