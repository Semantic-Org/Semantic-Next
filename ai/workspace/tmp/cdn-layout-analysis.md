## Key Takeaways

- **Universal (10/10):** Icon, Button, Heading, Text, and Container are needed in every single layout. These are the absolute foundation.
- **Near-universal (7-8/10):** Navbar, Checkbox, Input, Sidebar, and Dropdown appear in the vast majority of pages.
- **High-value (5-6/10):** Label, Card, Grid, Divider, Table, Breadcrumb, Skeleton, Avatar, Modal, and Pagination form the "workhorse" tier.
- **Contextual (3-4/10):** Tabs, Toast, Select, Link, Form, Form Field, Tooltip, Image, Upload, Empty State, and Statistic are important but layout-specific.
- **Specialized (1-2/10):** Components like Accordion, Carousel, Chart, Steps, Tree, etc. serve specific use cases but aren't broadly needed.
- **Unused (0/10):** Several components (Aspect Ratio, Autocomplete, Calendar, Color Picker, Command Palette, etc.) didn't appear in these 10 common layouts, suggesting they serve niche or specialized use cases.
